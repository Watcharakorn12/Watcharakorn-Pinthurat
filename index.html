Academic profile

ผศ.ดร.วัชรากร ปิ่นธุรัตน์

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Rajamangala University of Technology Tawan-Ok, Thailand.

My research spans distributed control, battery energy storage, microgrids, and learning-based approaches for power systems. My publications investigate voltage regulation, power unbalance compensation, energy management, and the integration of renewable generation.

I received my PhD in Electrical Engineering (Power and Energy System) from the University of New South Wales in 2023, following a master's degree at the Asian Institute of Technology and a bachelor's degree at Ubon Ratchathani University.

Research interests

Connecting control theory, data-driven methods, and distributed energy resources.

01

Intelligent distribution networks

Multi-agent reinforcement learning and data-driven control for voltage regulation, power unbalance mitigation, and active distribution systems.

02

Distributed energy storage

Decentralized and distributed battery control, heterogeneous storage coordination, and energy management across AC and DC microgrids.

03

Renewable energy & converters

Grid-forming converter control, renewable generation integration, electric vehicle charging, and microgrid stability.
